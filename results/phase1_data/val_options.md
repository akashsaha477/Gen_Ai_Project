## Options

Minimum-support rule (PREREGISTRATION §3, draft): F enters the selection metric only if validation holds at least 100 F beats from at least 2 records.

### V1: validation = [115, 118, 201, 205, 223]

Keeps record 208 (which holds most of DS1's F beats) in training, so the generators and classifiers have F to learn from. Validation gets S from three records with substantial counts (201, 118, 223), V from four, and one record with no ectopy (115, the lowest-numbered DS1 record with no S, V or F beats) to check N specificity. Record 201 goes to validation, so no model or generator trains on the subject who also appears in the test set as record 202.

| part | records | N | S | V | F |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| train | 17 | 35,496 (77%) | 644 (68%) | 3,030 (80%) | 388 (93%) |
| validation | 5 | 10,370 (23%) | 300 (32%) | 758 (20%) | 27 (7%) |

- Validation S: 300 beats in 4 records; largest is record 201 with 43%.
- Validation F: 27 beats in 3 records; largest is record 223 with 52%.
- Training S: 644 beats in 12 records; largest is record 209 with 59%.
- Training F: 388 beats in 7 records; largest is record 208 with 96%.
- Selection metric under the minimum-support rule: `s_f1`.
- Record 201 (same subject as test record 202) is in validation.
